## 2024-03-24 - High Contrast UI and Keyboard Shortcuts
**Learning:** Raw colored text (like `cv2.putText` with just `(0, 255, 255)`) overlayed on a dynamic camera feed often fails WCAG contrast ratio requirements depending on the background lighting and colors. Additionally, hidden keyboard shortcuts (like `q` to quit, `c` to calibrate) are not discoverable to users, hurting usability and accessibility.
**Action:** Implemented a custom `draw_text_with_outline` function that renders a thick black outline behind the foreground text, ensuring minimum contrast ratios regardless of the underlying camera frame. Also added persistent, high-contrast on-screen hints for all interactive keyboard shortcuts at the top of the viewport (e.g., `[Q] Quit`, `[C] Calibrate`, `[SPACE] Next Step`) to improve discoverability.
