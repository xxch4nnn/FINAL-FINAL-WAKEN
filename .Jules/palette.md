## 2024-05-10 - Persistent High-Contrast UI Overlays for Discoverability
**Learning:** In computer vision applications with dynamic camera feeds, standard colored text overlays frequently become unreadable against varying background colors and lighting conditions, creating accessibility issues. Additionally, hidden keyboard shortcuts (e.g., 'q' to quit, 'c' to calibrate) lead to poor discoverability and user frustration when they are not documented on-screen.
**Action:** Always implement a `draw_text_with_outline` utility that renders a high-contrast background outline (e.g., black) behind all dynamic text to ensure WCAG contrast guidelines are met against any background. Furthermore, persistently display available keyboard shortcuts at the top of the viewport (e.g., at coordinate `(10, 30)`) to ensure critical controls are always discoverable.
