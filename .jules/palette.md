## 2026-04-07 - [Text Outlines on Camera Feeds]
**Learning:** Text rendered directly over live camera feeds can often blend into the background, severely reducing legibility and discoverability of important UI features and state.
**Action:** Always use text outlining techniques (e.g., drawing a thick outline before drawing the text fill) when overlaying UI elements on live camera feeds using OpenCV to ensure high contrast and accessibility. Also ensure critical keyboard shortcuts are rendered explicitly on screen.
