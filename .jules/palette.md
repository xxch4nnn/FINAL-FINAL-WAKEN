## 2024-05-04 - Persistent HUD & High-Contrast Text Overlays
**Learning:** In computer vision applications with variable-lighting camera feeds, standard colored text overlays (like `cv2.putText`) often become unreadable against certain backgrounds. Furthermore, hidden keyboard shortcuts (e.g., waiting for 'q' or 'ESC' without any UI indicator) lead to poor discoverability and a frustrating user experience when trying to exit or interact with the engine.
**Action:** Always implement a `draw_text_with_outline` function that draws a black outline behind HUD text to guarantee WCAG contrast compliance regardless of the camera feed's background. Additionally, explicitly render available keyboard shortcuts persistently at the top of the viewport (e.g., `(10, 30)`) to ensure immediate discoverability.
