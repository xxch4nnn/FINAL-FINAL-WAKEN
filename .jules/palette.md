## 2025-04-22 - OpenCV Live Feed Contrast Patterns
**Learning:** OpenCV's native `cv2.putText` lacks contrast handling against arbitrary and unpredictable live camera feeds. The existing text elements (like "State: HOVER" in `VisionEngine.py` and `main_runtime.py`) are rendered with thin green or red strokes that become invisible against similarly-colored or bright backgrounds. Additionally, keyboard shortcuts (like `q` to quit or `c` to calibrate) are entirely undocumented in the UI, failing basic accessibility discoverability standards.
**Action:** When working with direct frame buffering in OpenCV, explicitly render UI accessibility elements like keyboard shortcuts at the top of the frame (`y=30`). Instead of raw `cv2.putText`, always use a text-outline stroke (e.g. rendering black with `thickness+2` followed by the target color with `thickness`) to guarantee legibility against any background context.
