## 2024-04-05 - Live Camera Text Legibility and Discoverability
**Learning:** Raw text drawn on live camera feeds often suffers from low contrast against unpredictable backgrounds, making it inaccessible. Keyboard shortcuts are also undiscoverable if not explicitly shown.
**Action:** Always use a 'text outline' technique (thick dark stroke followed by thin bright fill) for OpenCV UI text. Explicitly render essential keyboard shortcuts on-screen.
